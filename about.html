About AI Heritage

Understanding the importance of cultural heritage preservation through AI technology

What is Cultural Heritage?

Cultural heritage is the legacy of physical artifacts and intangible attributes of a group or society that are inherited from past generations, maintained in the present, and preserved for the benefit of future generations. Cultural heritage includes tangible culture (such as buildings, monuments, landscapes, books, works of art, and artifacts), intangible culture (such as folklore, traditions, language, and knowledge), and natural heritage (including culturally significant landscapes, and biodiversity).

The protection and preservation of cultural heritage has become increasingly important in the face of climate change, urbanization, conflict, and natural disasters. UNESCO (United Nations Educational, Scientific and Cultural Organization) works to identify and protect cultural and natural heritage around the world considered to be of outstanding value to humanity.

AI in Heritage Preservation

Artificial intelligence is revolutionizing heritage preservation by providing advanced tools for damage detection, documentation, and analysis. AI-powered systems can:

  • Detect structural damage, cracks, and erosion in heritage monuments with high precision
  • Monitor changes over time through automated image analysis
  • Create 3D reconstructions and digital twins of heritage sites
  • Predict potential damage risks based on environmental factors
  • Assist in restoration planning with data-driven recommendations

Our Mission

AI Heritage is committed to democratizing heritage preservation through accessible AI technology. Our platform aims to:

  • Provide free and easy-to-use AI tools for heritage damage detection
  • Build a global database of heritage sites and their preservation status
  • Empower local communities to monitor and protect their cultural heritage
  • Support researchers and conservationists with data-driven insights
  • Raise awareness about the importance of cultural heritage preservation
